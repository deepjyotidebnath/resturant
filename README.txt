Spice Route Kitchen - digital restaurant
1. Open index.html in a browser (or upload the whole folder to any web host).
2. Edit the R config at the top of assets/js/app.js (name, WhatsApp number, menu, hours, gallery).
3. Replace the 7 files in assets/images/ with your photos (same names, or update the gallery list in app.js).
4. After hosting, open the QR section on your live site and download one QR per table.
